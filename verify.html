Verify You're a Voter

Anyone can read every deliberation. Only registered voters in the district can vote or add statements, so every voice is a real, verified voter. Verifying means two checks: that a voter record exists for you, and that you're really that voter. In the live version, it's designed so your votes can't be traced back to you.

Sample. No real check happens yet. Codes are shown on screen instead of mailed, and nothing you enter is stored or sent to Civic Accord.
