Civic Accord · ask for it

Ask Your Representatives

Ask every office that represents you to offer a public way for residents to work with them, built so anyone can check the results. It takes about two minutes. You review the message and send it yourself.

Step 1

Your address

Your address goes from your browser straight to the U.S. Census Bureau to find your districts. It is never sent to or stored by Civic Accord.

Pass it on

Share so others can ask theirs

Each person who opens your link writes to their own representatives. So the more it's shared, the more offices hear it directly. This site doesn't track you. Sharing sites have their own policies.
