Civic Accord · pack builder

Start a Deliberation

Set up a deliberation on any issue, for any government. You fill in the facts, the statements people vote on, and what each option would cost. The tool checks the rules as you go, and you can preview it before it goes for review.

How it goes live. A second person reviews every pack before residents see it. Your draft is saved only in this browser until you send it for review.

Pack checks

1Government

Used for per-resident costs

Rules from this government's law

The tool applies only the rules this government's own law or chamber rules set, with a source for each. Where no law applies, it adds none of its own.

Checks every rule above against its source before launch and each election cycle

2Issue

3Shared facts

What everyone reads before voting. Every fact needs a source link.

4Statements to vote on

Write each as one clear idea that people on different sides could honestly agree or disagree with. Aim for 6 to 12.

5Costs

Put a price on statements that would cost money. Every cost needs a short explanation of how it was estimated.

6Ways to pay (optional)

Fees, fines, or other revenue tied to a statement. If you can't estimate the amount reliably, mark it as not yet estimated.

7Preview and send for review

To send it for review, give the pack file to your local host, or open an issue on the project's GitHub and attach it. A reviewer checks sources, costs, and neutral wording before it opens.
